Inference is at the cell level (the unit that does not repeat seeds): removing either load-bearing component hurts in all six cells (sign test $p{=}0.031$, the six-cell floor), while the $\rho$ schedule and lambda freeze split the cells and are cc-F1-neutral on these already-satisfied cells. \del{Two essential ingredients plus two neutral ones, \emph{not} four co-equal components.}
{\color{blue}The reset is the only component isolated singly: the second row removes the
reset \emph{and} the hinge together, via the TraLO-bounded control, which strips both
(Sec.~\ref{sec:setup}); this is why removing two components costs less than removing the
reset alone, and it means the hinge's individual contribution is not identified here. The
final row is an \emph{addition}, not a removal, and is reported for completeness: restoring
the KL anchor on these cells improves cc-F1 significantly, against the direction of the
main text's treatment of KL (App.~\ref{app:ablation}).}}
