\caption{\textbf{Model quality at the tight $L30$ cap} (MobileNetV3; each value is the mean over seeds 1--4 \emph{at this single cap level}). All methods meet the count; the post-hoc clippers do so by \emph{editing predictions}, and Flips is the number of test predictions overwritten to enforce the cap. This editing erodes \textbf{macro-F1}, the imbalance-aware quality metric, on every dataset. Raw \emph{accuracy} is nearly blind to it: the constrained class is rare, so clipping it barely moves the aggregate and the clippers can tie or even beat the trained methods on accuracy while losing macro-F1. This is why F1, not accuracy, is the right quality metric here. $^{\dagger}$ = post-hoc clipping. \textbf{Bold} = best, \underline{underline} = 2nd-best per column within each dataset block.}
