All estimates and intervals are paired, seed-level **constrained-class F1 percentage points**. Holm adjustments are within each block's two fixed primary contrasts. The scorer checked each block's architecture, complete summaries, matching step/sham radius and cap landing at every epoch, exact cap fill in every deployed arm, and unique PTO prediction vectors. Queue logs have one exit-0 END record per planned seed and no failed END record. The fmow2 pilot also confirms its applied step in all seven pilot epochs. These are development-pool outcomes; neither the 1,656-image Chen test nor fmow2's five reserved countries were scored.

The readings fixed before launch are clear. MobileNetV3 **clears both knee contrasts**, adding about 1.07 correct grade-3 slots of 76 over the ensembled clipper. EfficientNet-B5 **reverses direction**: the step loses about 0.79 slot, despite using Yuval Kassif's own backbone and the same pipeline. The satellite-image block **does not establish transfer**: both primary intervals include zero and Holm-adjusted p is 0.308. There is no pooling across backbones or datasets. The ResNet18 and RegNetY preregistered joint result remains intact, but the extended evidence rules out a universal-backbone or cross-dataset claim.

Secondary effects matter for deployment. On knee MobileNetV3, E2 accuracy is +0.87 [ +0.63, +1.11 ], macro-F1 +0.93 [ +0.67, +1.19 ], and weighted-F1 +0.46 [ +0.23, +0.69 ]. On B5, E2 accuracy is −0.22 [ −0.70, +0.25 ], macro-F1 **−2.00** [ −2.65, −1.34 ], and weighted-F1 **−2.06** [ −2.63, −1.49 ]. On fmow2, E2 accuracy is **−1.37** [ −1.63, −1.12 ], macro-F1 **−0.93** [ −1.15, −0.71 ], and weighted-F1 **−1.15** [ −1.41, −0.89 ]. These secondary metrics were prespecified for reporting but do not form a pooled family claim.

The snapshot ensemble itself remains useful: `ens_pto − pto` is +1.71 [ +1.16, +2.26 ] on knee MobileNetV3, +2.43 [ +1.56, +3.29 ] on B5, and +3.56 [ +3.12, +4.00 ] on fmow2. A same-radius random move adds approximately zero to that ensemble in all three blocks. Thus the train-time constraint direction has a small, context-dependent effect on top of a stronger label-free averaging baseline.

Scope and next gate: this is a single capped class, transductive step applied to side copies of each epoch model, then a fixed snapshot average and exact post-hoc allocation. The main PTO training trajectory is unchanged. Repeated use of the same knee development pool for method exploration limits external validity, even though these seed blocks and readings were fixed in advance. The fmow2 ResNet18 expansion visible as **uncommitted work** in the active worktree is not part of these results; it needs its own finished protocol, validation and compute decision before any run. Preserve all negative results and do not choose a different cap, window, epoch or backbone after seeing these outcomes.
