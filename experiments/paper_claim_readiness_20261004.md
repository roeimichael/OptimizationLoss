# Paper-claim readiness audit, 4 October 2026

**Verdict:** the codebase has reproducible, bounded *development* findings, but it does not yet support an unqualified paper claim that training-time dual-constraint TraLO beats a matched post-hoc clipper. This is an audit of existing evidence, not a new experimental protocol or permission to open a sealed evaluation. Preserve the negative and null cells alongside the positive one.

The knee effects are a **snapshot side-copy intervention**, not a demonstration that a training-time TraLO loss or a jointly active pooled-and-local constraint improves a model. The existing [paper-facing results draft](tralo_paper_results_draft_20260930.md) states that boundary and reports the secondary tradeoffs. The two positive knee backbones originally designated as the thesis pair have significant weighted-F1 losses, so they do not justify an all-metric or non-dominated win under `RULESET.md`. The negative B5 and fMoW cells rule out a universal-backbone or transfer claim. The local calibrated results do not establish a positive dual-constraint claim. A seed interval measures variation over training seeds on these fixed pools; it is not uncertainty over unseen patients, sites, or countries.

## Gates that remain open

1. **Scientific target.** The maintained framework asks whether *training-time*, matched dual-constraint TraLO beats a post-hoc clipper on allocated cc-F1 without being dominated on the rest of the profile. The positive knee snapshot study does not test that full question. Do not silently rename its treatment as a training-time loss.
2. **Independent evaluation.** The Chen knee test and reserved fMoW countries have not been scored. Development labels have informed prior study interpretation; further seeds on the same pools are exploratory. A sealed evaluation needs a frozen claim, candidate, contrasts, allocator, group/cap policy, secondary tradeoff rule, and analysis plan before one opening. The project rules require a user decision for held-out access.
3. **New dataset design.** HAM10000's official images and metadata pass source-integrity checks, but its proposed MEL-versus-rest, lesion-disjoint 60/10/30 cell changes the scientific question and lacks patient IDs. Its earlier candidate split failed the unchanged local-group support gate. The [prospective design memo](ham10000_prospective_design_for_review_20261004.md) is unapproved. ISIC 2024 still lacks a frozen patient-disjoint split, weak-label review and the remaining data/training gates.
4. **Finite compute.** The verified nonoverlapping spend lower bound is **72.175499 aggregate GPU-hours** of the 96-hour planning ceiling. At most **23.824501** hours remain mathematically; the spendable balance is not certified because smokes, scoring and other attempts are unreconciled. No new GPU booking follows from this lower bound.
5. **Manuscript.** The old TeX entry point was incomplete and is preserved under `report/archive/`. A new buildable evidence report is at `report/main.tex`; it is a claim-readiness record, not a finished paper. A publishable methods/results section must disclose data exposure, transductive image access, source identities, exact arms and dose, all prespecified comparisons, four-backbone heterogeneity, secondary harms and the absence of held-out confirmation.

The next gated decision is a **single concrete claim and untouched evaluation protocol**, made before accessing sealed labels. Until then, the defensible wording is that a label-free, transductive snapshot step improved allocated grade-3 F1 on three knee development backbones at cap 76, reversed on B5, and did not yield a demonstrated positive local dual-constraint result on the other completed datasets. That wording is narrower than the project's main paper bar.
