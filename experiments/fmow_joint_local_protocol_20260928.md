# Fixed development study: fmow2 pooled and country ceilings

Status: implementation and validation in progress. **No GPU run has been launched.** This protocol must be frozen in an immutable release before the first pilot. A failed validation gate stops dispatch without substituting a favorable cap, seed, backbone or country.

## Question and prior evidence

Does a label-free TraLO step aimed at pooled **and** country ceilings improve the *identity* of class-1 items selected under the same deployment policy, compared with the pooled-only direction at matched dose? This tests a new local mechanism, not a general claim from the knee result. The completed fmow2 MobileNetV3 pooled-only step ensemble had a +0.24 percentage-point cc-F1 difference versus its snapshot-only control (interval −0.09 to +0.57), with worse accuracy, macro-F1 and weighted-F1. The old 252-run fmow2 size-share local-cap campaign was negative. Both results remain part of the evidence.

## Data, budgets and grouping

Use the existing byte-audited fmow2 arrays and country-disjoint roles in `tralo/fmow_yuval.py`: 15,841 training items from 125 countries, 1,829 early-stop items from 14 other training countries, 1,673 development items in IRQ/NLD/DZA/PHL/TUR. The five other original test countries are excluded from this campaign. They were used in older analyses of the full fmow2 test pool, so they are **not** a globally untouched confirmation set. No new data are downloaded. The full label file is hashed, then memory-mapped; only development-country label indices are materialized for later scoring. Pool images and country IDs can enter the transductive step; no development labels can enter training, quotas, allocation, early stopping or model selection.

Constrain class 1 (`crop_field`) at two prespecified pooled ceilings: `G = floor(N/10) = 167` and `G = floor(N/20) = 83`. For each G, define geographic slack `B = ceil(5G/4)`, hence B=209 and B=104. Apportion B over the five countries in proportion to their **unlabeled pool sizes**, by Hamilton largest-remainder rounding with country-code tie break. These local ceilings sum to B, which exceeds G, so neither pooled nor local limits are automatically implied by the other. The 25% slack represents tolerance for geographic imbalance while retaining a 10% or 5% pooled resource ceiling; it is a research policy, not a known external entitlement. Alternatives were equal total country shares (would make the pooled cap redundant), prevalence-based shares (leak held-out labels), and no local ceilings (the control). Do not revise B or the allocation after any development score is read.

Deployment policy: one-class `local_capped_first`. From all items, select the highest class-1 probabilities subject to both each country ceiling and G, breaking ties by sample ID. Assign unselected items to their best uncapped class. Save raw calls separately. Every arm uses exactly this allocator and the same country IDs and ceilings. Audit exact counts and compare small instances with exhaustive enumeration. A cap is an upper bound, so an underfilled cap must be reported rather than silently forced full.

## Model, arms and outcome

Use the same MobileNetV3-Large Yuval pipeline and stopping rule as the completed fmow2 block, with one PTO training trajectory per seed and FP32. Save the PTO probability snapshot each epoch. On side copies of that epoch model, compute for **each G**:

1. `ens_pto`: mean of the untouched PTO snapshots, then joint allocation.
2. `ens_joint`: mean of snapshots after the new pooled-plus-country step, then joint allocation.
3. `ens_global_dose`: pooled-only soft-count direction moved by the joint step's exact parameter radius on the same PTO snapshot, then joint allocation. It may be infeasible *before* allocation; the allocator is common.
4. `ens_sham`: random step with the joint step's per-tensor displacement norms on the same snapshot, then joint allocation.

The new step activates scopes by **hard** over-calls. Its declared heuristic direction is the negative gradient of the sum of active soft class-1 counts divided by `max(cap,1)` for each scope. This weights violations by their declared capacity; it is neither a proven common-descent direction nor a globally optimal projection. Compute each active scope's exact parameter-space directional derivative and reject a direction that fails to descend in any scope. Record a near-origin soft-count replay as a diagnostic, without treating its finite change as the derivative. Conflicting scopes are a recorded method failure. Search at fixed radii from 0.001 up to 0.1 in parameter L2 norm, then a fixed 24-point scan from zero to the first feasible upper radius. Accept only a radius whose exact hard recount meets **all** ceilings; otherwise retain the failure and stop the pilot. This bounded scan does not claim the mathematically smallest feasible radius. The sham and pooled-only direction use the joint radius, without an additional feasibility search. An independent finite-difference gradient test, conflicting-scope fixture, full-vs-chunked replay parity and dose audit are required before a GPU pilot.

Use the existing fixed ensemble window `max(1, best_epoch-2)..last_epoch` for every arm. No arm gets extra checkpoint selection or training. The first primary contrast is paired development class-1 deployed F1 (`ens_joint - ens_global_dose`) at each cap. The second is `ens_joint - ens_pto` at each cap, to price added benefit over the snapshot clipper. Report all four contrasts as one family with Holm correction. A positive claim requires both contrasts positive after correction at a cap and no domination on accuracy, macro-F1 or weighted-F1; the other cap and all negative secondary effects remain visible. Report all seed deltas, seed SD and paired intervals. Raw predicted counts, per-country quota utilization and true positives, exact slot turnover, timing and failed steps are mechanism diagnostics, not quality wins.

## Execution and stopping

Before GPU dispatch, exercise the independent full-seed scorer against runner-shaped CPU artifacts to catch schema errors. The pilot gate itself is label-free; pilot scores may not change any fixed settings.

Measure pilot GPU time per complete seed and project the 48-seed GPU-hours before expansion. The fixed study has a ceiling of 288 GPU-hours (four cards for three days). If the projection exceeds that ceiling, stop expansion and seek a mathematically equivalent speed improvement with fresh parity checks; do not reduce seeds, epochs, caps, arms or integrity checks based on pilot scores. The launch gate uses exact per-scope directional derivatives; the near-origin soft-count replay is only a diagnostic.

Pilot seed 6099 is for integrity only; full fixed seeds are 6100–6147 (48 independent PTO fits), never overlapping previous fmow2 blocks. Both caps share each PTO trajectory and seed. Pilot metrics cannot be used to change caps, country policy, step radius, ensemble window, or model. The full block launches only if source/data/split hashes, country and sample alignment, finite gradients, exact per-scope directional descent, same-radius controls, exact quota feasibility, equal PTO trajectory, artifact hashes, and independent metric recomputation pass. If a fixed pilot reveals no local violation or no jointly feasible radius, report a non-informative or failed mechanism; do not launch a replacement with more favorable settings.

Before any GPU use, inspect actual UUID→PID→owner on **both** hosts and respect other users and scheduler limits. Use a fresh immutable release and exclusive outputs. Pilot on at most two free GPUs, inspect first results, then expand only under the validated fixed protocol to the number of genuinely free authorized cards. Host and precision differences are recorded as separate conditions; do not pool them silently. If SSH is unavailable, job state is unknown and no launch or duplicate recovery is allowed. The saved prior snapshots are probability tensors, not epoch model checkpoints; they support CPU allocation checks but cannot supply new epoch-wise model gradients. No existing fit is counted twice.

Evidence boundary: this is exploratory development evaluation on the same five countries previously viewed for the pooled-only study. Even a positive 48-seed result does not establish a new-dataset or held-out claim. An independent patient/source-disjoint confirmation requires a later, separately fixed design.
