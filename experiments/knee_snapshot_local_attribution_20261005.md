# Approved overnight knee snapshot attribution pilot

The human approved this pilot on October 5, then authorized execution, further
experiments and readiness simulations. Execution remains conditional on the
existing data, derivative, logging, source, ownership and certified budget gates.
Approval does not add to the 96 aggregate GPU-hour ceiling. This is exploratory
development evidence: earlier studies have already viewed the Chen development
outcomes. Chen test data remain sealed.

## Question and fixed design

Does adding artificial local constraints change the useful snapshot correction
on the same knee task when Yuval's successful training core is preserved?
The existing official train/development roles and five-grade target remain;
grade 3 has a pooled limit of 76. The classifier consumes RGB images only.

Every subject receives group `H0` or `H1` from the low bit of the integer SHA256
digest of `tralo-knee-artificial-local-20261005:` followed by its seven-digit
subject ID. Both knees therefore stay together. One namespace is used once;
no alternative partitions are searched. The 95 local slots are apportioned
by development image group size using the existing Hamilton rule and
lexicographic tie breaking. Groups, counts and quotas use no evaluation targets.
If either group is absent, preparation refuses rather than changing the hash.

Prospective scientific seeds are 7001–7004, subject to a cross-root freshness
check and exclusive claims before any execution. Reservation in this document
does not certify historical non-use, claim a seed, or authorize budget spend.
No extra seeds, retries of claimed seeds, or early stopping of the panel based
on quality are allowed. Failed/incomplete attempts remain evidence.

Use MobileNetV3-Large with the existing ImageNet V2 initialization, FP32, Yuval
RGB augmentation, balanced sampling with replacement, Adam at 1e-4, zero weight
decay, batch 32, learning-rate factor .8 every five epochs, at most 75 epochs,
and patience five. Early stopping uses only the existing train-subject carve:
SHA256(subject) modulo ten equals zero. This retains the snapshot core but
explicitly differs from the historical knee studies' 1e-4 weight decay.
Development inference batches contain 16 images. No shortening or subsampling
is permitted merely to fit cost; an unaffordable recipe fails the cost gate.

## Matched arms and policies

Train one supervised PTO trajectory. At each epoch all corrections act only
on isolated model copies; the original model, gradients, mode and RNG must
remain unchanged. Side steps never feed into the next supervised epoch.
Keep these six prediction arms:

1. PTO: unmodified epoch model.
2. Global-native: existing pooled hard-count-triggered bracket/bisection step.
3. Global-native sham: the same native radius and per-tensor displacement norms
   in an independently seeded random direction.
4. Joint-local: existing active normalized pooled/local soft-count direction,
   common-descent check and finite hard-feasibility scan, ceiling .1, initial
   radius .001, 30 doubling attempts and 24 scan points.
5. Global-at-joint-radius: pooled direction at the joint arm's radius, activated
   exactly when that joint correction applies.
6. Joint sham: random direction at the joint arm's radius with its per-tensor
   displacement norms and matched activation.

An inactive joint step leaves both its matched controls at PTO. An infeasible,
nonfinite, zero-gradient or conflicting joint direction is recorded as a failed
attempt; no silent controller substitution, relaxation or discarded negative.
The native global and local radius algorithms differ. Their direct difference
alone cannot identify a local direction effect; arm 5 addresses that issue.
The global sham seed offset is 7 + 1000*epoch; the joint sham offset is
17 + 1000*epoch. These private generators do not consume training RNG.

Average the same snapshots for every arm: epochs max(1,best_epoch-2) through
the last epoch run, matching the earlier snapshot study. Cross all six averaged
probabilities through both the existing pooled capped-first policy and the
existing pooled-plus-local capped-first policy. Each policy is common to every
arm. Raw counts are diagnostic. Policies never consume labels.

Primary estimands are joint minus PTO, joint minus joint sham, joint minus
global-at-joint-radius, global-native minus PTO, and global-native minus its
sham, separately under each policy. Report all ten paired differences with
individual seed values, paired t(3) intervals and Holm-adjusted two-sided
p-values across the ten declared comparisons. Report accuracy, weighted F1,
macro F1, group metrics and feasibility alongside capped-class F1. Four seeds
are a pilot, not a reliable universal superiority claim. Do not select a
controller, dose, group definition or new study from a favorable viewed result
without a separately declared design. If no gain survives uncertainty and
secondary tradeoffs, report that outcome plainly.

## Required readiness and interpretation

Preparation may read the authorized train and development folders, placing
development labels in a separate private scorer artifact. It must never
enumerate or decode the test tree. Runtime receives only neutral copied image
paths, development IDs and groups; no grade-bearing source paths or targets.
Logical separation is not OS access-control certification. Authenticate public
manifest/row/image bytes before runtime and deny private-file access in the
integration gate. The scoring role opens only the approved development labels
after the complete frozen panel; it does not open Chen test labels.

Before a GPU campaign: verify actual source data lineage/disjointness and image
reader routing; analytic/autograd and streamed parameter gradients; correction
displacements and sham parity; unchanged PTO model/gradients/optimizer trajectory
and RNG; complete event/artifact identity and failed-attempt logging; local and
both-server source identity; pinned pretrained bytes; actual precision/device;
finite full-recipe cost and dose; exclusive seed/device ownership; certified
remaining aggregate budget. A physical UUID request alone does not certify
ownership. Each job is bounded by six hours and the cell by 24 GPU hours, also
subject to the smaller certified aggregate remainder. Budget uncertainty blocks
dispatch, not CPU preparation.

Log pooled/local soft and hard counts, residuals, active scopes, gradient norms,
direction alignment, search probes/evaluations, radius, actual tensor and total
displacements, planned/attempted/applied/skipped updates, snapshot hashes and
ensemble window. Multipliers and augmentation coefficients are explicitly not
applicable to this normalized snapshot direction study. Random artificial
groups probe optimization mechanics, not clinical subgroup benefit or fairness.
Parallel pooled/joint directions or nonbinding local quotas can make the local
direction comparison uninformative; report this rather than changing groups.

The earlier knee gain and uncertain global-only fMoW transfer stay intact.
This pilot isolates the local intervention on knee under a fixed new no-decay
recipe; it cannot establish that dataset alone caused the prior transfer gap.
