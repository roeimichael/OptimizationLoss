# Independent knee confirmation: reviewable design, not an authorized run

**Status:** design only. No MOST data have been requested, downloaded, inspected, or scored. This design does not authorize use of the untouched 1,656-image Chen test split. Access to a new cohort and any compute for this evaluation require the project decision in `AGENTS.md` before execution.

## Decision this would answer

The OAI-derived Chen development split has been examined repeatedly. The fixed four-seed hard-pair study produced an active ranking gradient but no reliable gain in correct grade-3 occupancy of the exact 76 deployed slots over phase Null or Clipper. An evaluation on an independent cohort would test whether the *already trained* hard-rank-only models transfer to new patients and imaging practice; it would not validate a newly invented loss or prove clinical benefit. The primary contrast is hard-rank-only versus its phase-matched Null. Ordinary Clipper is a required practical anchor. Rank-plus-count versus rank-only is secondary, with count-only reported to expose raw-call suppression. All four existing seeds and all five arms are included, irrespective of Chen results. No retraining, checkpoint selection, calibration, or parameter sweep is part of this confirmation.

The preferred cohort is baseline (V0) knee radiographs from the Multicenter Osteoarthritis Study (MOST), which is separate from OAI. MOST Online says data require registration and a Data Use Agreement, with an authorization request containing an analysis-plan idea before registration. The NIA AgingResearchBiobank lists MOST knee radiographs and baseline KL readings. Sources: [MOST Online](https://mostonline.ucsf.edu/), [registration instructions](https://mostonline.ucsf.edu/Account/Register), [NIA MOST study](https://agingresearchbiobank.nia.nih.gov/studies/most/details), and [MOST knee X-ray reading protocol](https://agingresearchbiobank.nia.nih.gov/studies/most/documents/download/Dataset_Descriptions/DatasetDescription_ReadingProtocolMOSTKneeXRAY.pdf/). An OAI repackage would not be an independent cohort. We should not use later MOST visits: longitudinal repeats complicate patient independence, and the X-ray protocol notes selection rules at later visits.

## Deployment rule and utility

For a cohort of `N` eligible, unique patients, choose at most one baseline knee per patient, using the right knee when its image and standard integer KL label 0–4 are available, otherwise the left. The choice uses no model score or grade preference. Record missing-image and nonstandard/missing-grade exclusions by reason; no exclusions based on model confidence or correctness. The cohort is frozen by patient IDs, image hashes, label-field mapping, and a signed eligibility manifest **before** any model inference. If a bilateral image must be cropped, the image-to-knee orientation, crop coordinates, scaling and 224-pixel transform must be specified and checked on a fixed, label-blind image sample before scoring. The Chen preprocessing must be matched where feasible; if it cannot be matched, stop and report the incompatibility rather than choose a favorable crop. No outcome-driven site filtering.

Grade 3 is the only capped class. Preserve the original training-prevalence pressure rule without using MOST grade prevalence: `R = round(N * 757 / 5778)`, then `K = round(0.7 * R)`, with a documented half-even rounding convention as in the Chen protocol. On Chen development `N=826`, this yields `R=108`, `K=76`. Freeze `N`, `R`, and `K` before inference. This scales the capacity with cohort size and gives more than 76 slots if the eligible external cohort is larger; it is a deployment policy hypothesis, not a known clinical quota. Do not substitute the number of true MOST grade-3 cases for `R` or `K`.

The primary operational quantity is **correct grade-3 people in the top `K` assigned grade-3 slots**, `TP@K`, under the already named capped-first allocator and deterministic sample-ID tie rule. Because the number of slots and true grade-3 support are fixed across arms, grade-3 precision, recall and F1 move monotonically with `TP@K`. Report the actual support, precision, recall, F1, false occupants, and the number of correct entrants/exits relative to Null. Also report raw argmax grade-3 count/F1, upper-bound-only allocation, all-class macro-F1/accuracy, and feasibility so a rank gain cannot hide loss of raw recognition or another class. `TP@K` represents useful constrained-slot selection under an imposed capacity; it does not by itself quantify patient harm, referral costs, or optimal care.

For each of four matched seeds, compare `TP@K` of hard-rank-only with phase Null and with ordinary Clipper. Report all per-seed differences, the mean, and a seed-level interval as descriptive evidence, not a claim of four independent patient cohorts. Also give a patient-resampled paired uncertainty analysis on the fixed external cohort, preserving every arm's scores together within each resample. The secondary rank-plus-count versus rank-only contrast is always reported; no best-seed or best-arm selection. Treat all statistical intervals cautiously because MOST image preprocessing and the training cohort differ. A win requires a consistent improvement in allocated correct membership **without a material collapse in raw grade-3 recognition**; there is no post-hoc numerical threshold for “material.” Clinical utility would require an independently justified cost model, which this study does not supply.

## Frozen-model evaluation gates

1. Confirm access rights, baseline radiograph/label schema, patient IDs, usable standard integer KL grades, and overlap exclusion. Preserve the exact data-use terms and dataset version. If access or label comparability fails, record the blocker; do not replace the cohort silently.
2. Confirm all 20 model checkpoint hashes and the immutable runtime release `eac8db3c069217121a1ff3bba41cdd055bad9ea8` against the completed audited Chen campaign. No weights, batch-norm statistics, or training state may change during MOST inference.
3. Before label-aware scoring, test DICOM decoding, laterality, crop orientation, image-quality exclusions, deterministic transforms, and inference repeatability on a label-blind sample. Keep the crop protocol and sample IDs fixed. Do not use MOST labels to tune crops or choose models.
4. Score the frozen manifest once for every seed and arm. Save raw probabilities, raw predictions, both named allocated predictions, configuration and artifact hashes, model identity, sample order, and finite-value checks. Independently recompute `TP@K`, all reported metrics, quota feasibility, and per-image correct/wrong slot transitions from saved predictions. Preserve every failure and never overwrite a run directory.
5. Publish the full table even if negative. The original Chen test split stays sealed for a later final evaluation of a method and utility chosen without seeing it. A new cutoff-oriented loss needs its own fixed prediction for raw calls **and** exact-slot membership, matched Null and Clipper, and fresh training seeds before any new GPU campaign.

## Alternatives and unresolved decisions

Scoring the existing Chen test split now would use the only internal untouched split to adjudicate a loss family that has not yet shown a development advantage; it would no longer be available as an untouched final check. Repackaged OAI subsets may contain overlapping people and are unsuitable as independent confirmation. An external hospital collection could work if provenance, licenses, standard KL labels and patient IDs are documented, but none is currently confirmed available to this project. MOST is preferred for independence and grade comparability, subject to access and image-format verification. The person authorized to accept MOST's Data Use Agreement must request access and confirm the permitted analysis. The research decision is whether an external zero-shot confirmation of the existing methods, with the above capacity policy and operational utility, is the next use of effort; no external data or additional compute should be committed before that decision.
