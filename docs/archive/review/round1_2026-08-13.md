### 1. Fresh Review Evaluation

#### Itemized Deductions

**Pillar 1 — Methodological Soundness &amp; Mathematical Precision (−11)**

* **−4 [Soundness]: The "ALM" baseline is not an augmented Lagrangian, and the paper's structural argument for it is false.** §4 states "The term entering the loss keeps Fioretto-LDF's form, $\lambda_c S_c$" — i.e. the primal objective is *linear*. `src/methodologies/fioretto_alm/train.py` confirms it: *"Identical to fioretto_ldf EXCEPT the dual (multiplier) update."* An augmented Lagrangian is *defined* by a quadratic penalty in the primal; there is none here. What is implemented, $\lambda_c \leftarrow \max(0,\lambda_c+\eta r_c)+\mu_t\max(0,r_c)$, is a proportional–integral controller on the multiplier — the family of `stooke2020responsive`/`sohrabi2024pi`, both cited in §2 and neither benchmarked. Consequently §2's claim that "*Its quadratic penalty growth is structurally close to the $\rho$ ramp… both bound the constraint-term gradient after a first violation*" is doubly wrong: there is no quadratic penalty, and a $\lambda$ augmented by $\mu_t r_c$ each epoch *grows* the constraint-term gradient rather than bounding it. Citing `bertsekas1982constrained` for this rule is a misattribution.
* **−3 [Notation]: two live symbol collisions.** In §3, $K_g$ is a *group* cap (Problem paragraph) while $\lambda_g$ in Eq. (3) is the *global* multiplier — and the very next sentence sums $\sum_g \lambda_{l,g}$ over groups. Separately, $c$ denotes the constrained class in the Problem paragraph and a cap index in the hinge paragraph and throughout App. A ($E_c, S_c, K_c$). Also Eq. (3) writes $\ell_g=\ell(E;K,\rho)$ with *unsubscripted* arguments for a per-cap quantity.
* **−2 [Design justification]: the hinge's safety argument does not hold as stated.** §3 says the hinge is "zero whenever the soft count sits at or above its cap, **so it can never re-violate**" and is "inert throughout the violated phase." Both are claims about the *soft* count, but feasibility is defined on the *hard* count (§3, Problem) — the paper's own acknowledged soft/hard gap. A soft count below $K$ with a hard count above $K$ is possible and unmeasured; in that state the hinge actively pushes the soft count *up* while the cap is still violated. Additionally, Algorithm 1 line 3 says the hinge is added "once satisfied" whereas the prose says it is always in the objective but inactive.
* **−2 [Assumptions]: App. A's finite-multiplier result rests on unflagged assumptions.** $c=|dL_{\rm CE}/dS|_{S=K}$ is assumed finite and $dL_{\rm CE}/dS\le 0$ is asserted "since the constrained class is under-served at any cap $L&lt;100\%$" — but App. G states that on TissueMNIST/DermMNIST "the warmup model already predicts the constrained class at close to its capped rate," which is exactly the regime where that sign assumption is weakest.
