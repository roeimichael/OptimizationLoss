## A2. Soft/hard count gap on knee -- the penalty fires on a feasible model

Null, 12 seeds, development pool:

| class | cap | true | hard (argmax) count | soft count (sum p) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| grade 3 | 82 | 106 | 74.8 | 111.0 |
| grade 4 | 16 | 27 | 9.6 | 23.7 |

The deployed argmax is ALREADY under both caps, but the soft count exceeds them by
35%/48%, so the bounded soft-count penalty keeps pushing p(grade 3) and p(grade 4)
down. That is the measured mechanism behind the end-to-end result (raw grade-3 calls
95.75 -> 26): the objective asks for less probability mass, not a better ordering, and
capped_first then refills the slots anyway. On CIFAR soft and hard counts agree
(e.g. 18.6 vs 18.0), which is consistent with CIFAR being the only place a small
positive signal appears.
