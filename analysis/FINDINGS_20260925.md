## A2. Soft/hard count gap on knee -- the penalty fires on a feasible model

Null, 12 seeds, development pool:

| class | cap | true | hard (argmax) count | soft count (sum p) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| grade 3 | 82 | 106 | 74.8 | 111.0 |
| grade 4 | 16 | 27 | 9.6 | 23.7 |

The deployed argmax is ALREADY under both caps, but the soft count exceeds them by
35%/48%, so the bounded soft-count penalty keeps pushing p(grade 3) and p(grade 4)
down. That is the measured mechanism behind the end-to-end result (raw grade-3 calls
95.75 -> 26): the objective asks for less probability mass, not a better ordering, and
capped_first then refills the slots anyway. On CIFAR soft and hard counts agree
(e.g. 18.6 vs 18.0), which is consistent with CIFAR being the only place a small
positive signal appears.

## A3. augfin (main branch, fmow2, LOCAL + global policy caps) -- scored and slot-analysed

Pre-registered outcome 2, AMBIGUOUS: aug_tralo - aug_clip cc_f1 +0.0011 / +0.0033

| cap | slots | reseed floor (same arm, s vs s') | aug_tralo vs its null | d_correct [95% t] |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L25_G25 | 307 | **0.50** | 0.33 (0.65x floor) | +0.58 [-3.42, +4.59] |
| L75_G75 | 924 | **0.38** | 0.24 (0.62x floor) | +2.42 [-5.41, +10.24] |

**Half of WHICH items get the capped slots is decided by the training seed.** The
method effect lives inside that noise. aug_tralo and aug_tralo_null share the warm-up
and the seed, so their 33% turnover is produced by ~10 constraint steps amplified
chaotically through end-to-end training. The frozen-head sweep does not show this
(1e-5 moves 0.1% of slots): a linear head on fixed features is not chaotic.

**Consequence for design.** A matched null separates "constraint on/off" but cannot
separate "the constraint's information" from "any perturbation of equal size". The
missing control is a SHAM constraint -- same steps, same step norm, target carrying
no information (budget-permuted caps, or a random-direction step of equal norm). If
TraLO's d_correct does not beat the sham's, the effect is perturbation, not constraint.
